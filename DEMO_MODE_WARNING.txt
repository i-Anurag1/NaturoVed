NATUROVED DEMO MODE

The current disease model may have random weights and has not been validated
on a correctly labeled, held-out crop-disease dataset.

Predictions, confidence scores, recommendations and Grad-CAM heatmaps must
not be treated as reliable crop-disease diagnoses until training and evaluation
on representative labeled images are complete.

A Grad-CAM image only demonstrates the visualization pipeline. It does not
prove that the model learned meaningful disease features.
